Psst.

Privacy policy. Last updated September 30, 2026.

Psst is made by Zigao Wang, who is responsible for the data described here.

Psst has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. It doesn't use any analytics or advertising services, and it never sells or shares anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places, the places you've already seen in the feed, your filters and settings, and any translations the app makes all stay on your device. If you let Psst use your location, it's used on the device to show places near you and is never sent anywhere.

What the app sends

Downloading stories and photos. The app downloads place and story files, and photos of places, from our server. Photos are copies we host ourselves, so viewing one doesn't contact the site it came from. The server doesn't record your IP address: its logs keep only the time, the file requested, and whether it worked, for 14 days. To stop abuse, the server counts requests from each address over the last minute. That count is kept in memory only, never written down, and forgotten within minutes.

Coverage requests. When you look at a city-sized part of the map where Psst has no stories yet, the app sends which area it was: the ID of a hexagon of about 250 square kilometers around the middle of the view. That's all it sends: no coordinates, no identifier, no account. It never sends where you are: while your own location is inside the map view, nothing is sent. Each phone sends a given hexagon at most once a day, and at most 10 a day. We keep only a daily count for each hexagon, to decide where to research next. You can turn this off in Settings, under "Help choose new areas".

Problem reports. If you report a problem with a story, we receive which story it was, the reason you picked, anything you chose to type, and the app version. Reports aren't linked to you. Each phone can report a story once, and send up to 5 reports a day. Please don't include personal information in the message.

Other services

Maps and street-level imagery come from Apple Maps, and translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. Apple's privacy policy covers those. Links to sources, and photo credits, open in your browser, and the sites you visit have their own policies.

Children

Psst doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
