Psst.

Privacy policy. Last updated September 29, 2026.

Psst is made by Zigao Wang, who is responsible for the data described here.

Psst has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. It doesn't use any analytics or advertising services, and it never sells or shares anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places, the places you've already seen in the feed, your filters and settings, and any translations the app makes all stay on your device. If you let Psst use your location, it's used on the device to show places near you and is never sent anywhere.

What the app sends

Downloading stories. The app downloads place and story files from our server. Like any website, the server's access logs record your IP address and the files requested. These logs are kept for 14 days for security and then deleted.

Coverage requests. When you look at a city-sized part of the map where Psst has no stories yet, the app sends the middle of that map area, rounded to about 10 kilometers. It isn't your location, and nothing else is sent: no identifier. We turn it into a hexagon of about 250 square kilometers and keep only a daily count for each hexagon, to decide where to research next. You can turn this off in Settings, under "Help choose new areas".

Problem reports. If you report a problem with a story, we receive which story it was, the reason you picked, anything you chose to type, and the app version. Reports aren't linked to you. Please don't include personal information in the message.

Other services

Maps and street-level imagery come from Apple Maps, and translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. Apple's privacy policy covers those. Links to sources open in your browser, and the sites you visit have their own policies.

Children

Psst doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Email a@zigao.wang. You can also ask for any problem report you sent to be deleted; include the story and roughly when you sent it, since reports aren't linked to you.
